# Mock interview rubric

Score each domain 1–5 and record a concrete example.

1. **Problem framing:** clarifies audience, decision, population, grain and denominator.
2. **SQL/data reasoning:** explains joins, aggregation, nulls, keys and query validation.
3. **Python/Excel execution:** describes reproducible workflow and quality controls.
4. **Healthcare interpretation:** distinguishes order/administration/dispensing/adherence and proxy/outcome.
5. **Analytical judgement:** reports uncertainty and limitations; avoids causal overclaim.
6. **Communication:** concise, structured and understandable to non-technical stakeholders.
7. **Remote collaboration:** written updates, escalation, versioning and handoff.
8. **Integrity:** clearly separates academic, synthetic portfolio and employment experience.

A good answer usually follows: **context → task → method → validation → result → limitation → decision**.

Do not memorise scripts. Practise explaining the reasoning and adapting when assumptions change.
