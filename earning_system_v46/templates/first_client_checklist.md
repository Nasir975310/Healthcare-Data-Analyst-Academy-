# First-client readiness checklist

- [ ] Client identity and authority verified.
- [ ] Business question and intended user documented.
- [ ] Data owner and sharing authority confirmed.
- [ ] PHI/security classification and approved environment agreed.
- [ ] Scope, exclusions, grain and metric definitions signed off.
- [ ] Deliverables and acceptance criteria agreed.
- [ ] Timeline, revision limits and change control agreed.
- [ ] Fee, invoice and payment terms agreed in writing.
- [ ] Backup, retention, deletion and handoff process agreed.
- [ ] No unsupported clinical, causal, regulatory or financial claims.
- [ ] Final deliverables independently reproduced and reviewed.
