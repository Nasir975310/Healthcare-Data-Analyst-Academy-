# Weekly application execution workflow

1. Collect vacancies from employer career pages and reputable job boards.
2. Record employer, exact title, URL, date found, deadline, location, remote conditions, pay transparency and source.
3. Run the v5.56 job-fit evaluator and then generate the v5.57 interview pack.
4. Manually confirm mandatory criteria, eligibility, experience, shifts and location.
5. Tailor the CV by reordering truthful evidence; never add unsupported keywords.
6. Submit only through a verified channel. Never pay to obtain a job or share OTPs/passwords.
7. Record application date, CV version, contact, stage, next action and outcome.
8. Review weekly: applications, response rate, screening rate, interview rate, rejection reasons and skill gaps.

These metrics are operational feedback, not proof of qualification or causation.
