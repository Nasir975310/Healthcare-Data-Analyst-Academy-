# Healthcare analytics case studies

Eight structured cases cover inpatient operations, antihypertensive DUE, pharmacovigilance, claims-based adherence proxies, RWE cohort design, clinical trial data quality, emergency flow and laboratory turnaround time.

For each case, produce a short protocol/metric definition, reproducible analysis, data-quality evidence, interpretation, limitations and stakeholder handoff. The brief supplies a scenario, analytical approach, deliverables and guardrails. Dataset-specific starter files are not implied unless explicitly listed in the relevant Academy project.

All examples are educational. Use synthetic data only. Do not infer causality from observational comparisons, medication ingestion from order/claims records, or regulatory causality from disproportionality screening.
