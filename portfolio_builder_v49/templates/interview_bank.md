# Project-based interview preparation

1. What was the dataset grain and how did you identify it?
2. Which data-quality defects were planted and how did you detect them?
3. Why quarantine invalid rows instead of silently correcting them?
4. How did you verify raw = accepted + quarantined?
5. How did you prevent duplicate keys in SQLite?
6. Explain the SQL query used to calculate department-level encounter counts.
7. What does mean length of stay represent, and what does it not represent?
8. How would case mix and confounding affect department comparisons?
9. How would you adapt the pipeline for a larger dataset?
10. What security and governance controls would be required for real patient data?
11. Which tests would you add before production use?
12. What are the limitations of your synthetic dataset?
