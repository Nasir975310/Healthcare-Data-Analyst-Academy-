# Cohort and endpoint specification

## Cohort contract
Specify target population, setting, eligibility, exclusions, source, unit of analysis, index date, baseline look-back, exposure ascertainment, comparator, follow-up start/end, censoring and repeated-episode handling.

## Endpoint contract
For every endpoint specify construct, operational definition, event source, numerator/denominator where relevant, time window, first/recurrent event rule, competing events, censoring, missing/indeterminate handling and reviewer.

## Avoid common errors
- Align time zero across comparison groups.
- Avoid defining exposure using information collected after follow-up begins unless the design explicitly handles it.
- Do not call an association causal without a defensible causal design and assumptions.
- Distinguish prevalence, incidence proportion, incidence rate and hazard.
- Distinguish participant-, encounter-, visit- and event-level grain.
- Prespecify subgroup analyses and multiplicity handling.
