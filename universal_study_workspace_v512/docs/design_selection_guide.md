# Selecting a study design

Choose the design from the research question and data-generating process, not convenience alone.

| Design | Typical question | Temporal feature | Common concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cohort | Does exposure precede outcome? | Index then follow-up | Confounding, immortal time, censoring |
| Case-control | Is prior exposure associated with case status? | Outcome-defined sampling | Control selection, recall/measurement bias |
| Cross-sectional | What is prevalence at a snapshot? | Concurrent measurement | Temporality, sampling and non-response |
| Randomized trial | What is the effect of assignment? | Random allocation then follow-up | Allocation concealment, estimand, adherence |
| Diagnostic accuracy | How well does a test classify a condition? | Index test vs reference standard | Spectrum and verification bias |
| Case series | What is observed in a defined case group? | Defined observation period | Selection and missing comparator |
| Registry/longitudinal | How do measures/events change? | Repeated visits or event times | Informative missingness and attrition |

This wizard is an educational planning aid, not a substitute for protocol review, ethics approval, trial registration or specialist statistical advice.
