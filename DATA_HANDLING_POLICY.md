# Data handling and synthetic-data policy

1. Training datasets are fictional/synthetic unless a module explicitly documents otherwise.
2. The academy is not a clinical system and must not be used to make patient-care, staffing, triage, or service-delivery decisions.
3. Do not upload, commit, or distribute identifiable patient information, confidential institutional data, or restricted research records.
4. De-identification does not automatically make a dataset safe to publish; assess re-identification risk, consent, institutional approvals, contracts, and applicable law.
5. Keep the user's Pharm.D thesis dataset separate from synthetic academy projects. Thesis findings must only be reported from the real study data and approved analysis.
6. Before publishing any module, inspect source files, data, notebooks, logs, screenshots, metadata, and Git history for sensitive information.
