# Report workflow and QA

1. Confirm data provenance, permission, reporting period, grain and inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Validate unique keys, date ranges, missingness, invalid values and duplicates.
3. State the unit of analysis and denominators for every KPI.
4. Separate observed results from interpretation and recommendations.
5. Record software/version, transformations, filters and source-file checksum.
6. Disclose limitations, small-cell risks, selection bias and missing data.
7. Independently reconcile headline counts to source data.
8. Remove direct identifiers and review disclosure risk before sharing.

**Interpretation guardrails:** descriptive reports do not establish causality, clinical validity, guideline concordance or patient-level recommendations. Synthetic demonstration values are not hospital performance.
