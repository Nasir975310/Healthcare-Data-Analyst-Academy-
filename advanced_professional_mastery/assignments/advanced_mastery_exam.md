# Advanced Professional Mastery Examination

**Duration:** 3 hours · **Total:** 100 points · **Suggested pass:** 75 points, with no critical integrity failure.

1. Advanced SQL investigation (25 points): grain, anti-joins, CTEs, windows, safe aggregation and query plan.
2. Statistical validation (25 points): cohort flow, crude/adjusted comparison, uncertainty and confounding.
3. Dashboard audit (20 points): metric reconciliation, join fan-out and corrected KPI definitions.
4. Reproducibility and QA (15 points): clean run, automated checks, environment and source manifest.
5. Executive communication (15 points): concise findings, decisions, limitations and next actions.

Critical failure: fabricated results, hidden exclusions, denominator manipulation, PHI/credential exposure, unauthorised data sharing or unsupported causal/clinical claims. This formative examination is not accredited.
