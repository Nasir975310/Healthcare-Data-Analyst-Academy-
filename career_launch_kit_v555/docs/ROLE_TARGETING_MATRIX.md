# Role Targeting Matrix

## Recommended order

| Priority | Role | Why it fits | Evidence to show | Main gaps to close |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Healthcare Data Analyst (junior) | Pharm.D domain knowledge plus SQL/Python/Excel learning path | Synthetic outpatient analytics project, metric contract, reproducible pipeline, thesis methods | Stronger SQL joins/window functions, Power BI dashboard, portfolio publication |
| 2 | Clinical Data Analyst / Clinical Data Coordinator | Familiarity with clinical variables, study design and data quality | Pharm.D thesis methods, data dictionary, validation rules, case-based analysis | EDC/CRF workflows, CDISC basics where relevant, query management |
| 3 | Clinical Research Coordinator / Data Coordinator | Direct alignment with prospective observational research | Thesis protocol, eligibility criteria, data collection and study documentation | GCP training, source documentation, site workflow exposure |
| 4 | Pharmacovigilance Associate | Pharm.D is directly relevant to drug safety and medication knowledge | Drug knowledge, adverse-event case reasoning, structured documentation | ICSR lifecycle, MedDRA, seriousness/expectedness, regulatory timelines |
| 5 | Healthcare Reporting / MIS Analyst | Accessible adjacent entry route | Excel reporting, SQL extracts, data quality checks, dashboard project | Advanced Excel, Power Query, Power BI, stakeholder reporting |
| 6 | RWE / HEOR Analyst (entry-level) | Clinical research foundation and interest in observational data | Study design, descriptive statistics, confounding awareness | Epidemiology, causal inference, R, SQL, claims/EHR data structures |

## Screening rules

Prioritize jobs that:
- Accept 0–2 years of experience or fresh graduates.
- Explicitly value Pharm.D, pharmacy, life sciences or clinical research.
- Include Excel, SQL, data validation, clinical documentation or reporting.
- Have a credible employer and verifiable job description.

Treat as lower priority:
- Roles requiring 3–5+ years of production BI ownership.
- Jobs requiring advanced cloud/data engineering as a minimum.
- Vague work-from-home offers that request registration fees, deposits, paid training or sensitive identity/bank information before a legitimate offer.

## Application allocation

A practical weekly allocation after the CV is finalized:
- 40% primary healthcare/clinical analytics roles.
- 25% clinical research data roles.
- 20% pharmacovigilance roles.
- 15% adjacent reporting/MIS roles.

Adjust after 30–40 well-targeted applications based on response rate and role fit.
