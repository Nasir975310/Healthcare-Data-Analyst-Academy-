# Portfolio Project Summary — Evidence-Based Version

## Project 1: Synthetic Outpatient Operations Analytics

**Purpose:** Demonstrate an end-to-end operational analytics workflow using fictional outpatient encounter records.

**Tools:** Python standard library, SQL, CSV, automated tests and GitHub Actions workflow configuration.

**Work performed:** Defined record grain and metric rules; removed one duplicate; applied eligibility and wait-time validity checks; generated department-level summaries and quality exceptions; documented denominators and limitations.

**Reference results:** 24 raw rows, 23 after duplicate removal, 20 eligible completed encounters and 18 valid wait-time observations. Mean waits: Cardiology 21.29 minutes (n=7), General Medicine 32.60 minutes (n=5), Orthopaedics 33.67 minutes (n=6).

**Limitations:** Synthetic teaching data; small groups; descriptive only; not representative of a real hospital and not suitable for operational decisions.

## Project 2: Synthetic Antihypertensive Utilization Case Study

**Purpose:** Practice medication-utilization analysis and clinical reporting on fictional records.

**Tools:** Python, SQL, statistical methods and reproducible reporting.

**Work performed:** Data-quality checks, utilization summaries, paired blood-pressure analysis, sensitivity analysis and documented interpretation guardrails.

**Limitations:** Entirely synthetic. It is separate from the user's Pharm.D thesis and does not establish drug efficacy or causal effects.
