# Career Launch Kit — v5.55.0

A practical job-search execution pack for a Pharm.D graduate/near-graduate targeting entry-level healthcare data, clinical data and adjacent analytics roles. It emphasizes evidence-backed claims and separates academic research from synthetic portfolio demonstrations.

## Target role sequence

1. Healthcare Data Analyst / Clinical Data Analyst — primary target.
2. Clinical Research Data Coordinator / Clinical Data Associate — strong domain-adjacent target.
3. Pharmacovigilance Associate / Drug Safety Associate — Pharm.D-aligned target; analytics skills are a differentiator.
4. Healthcare Reporting Analyst / MIS Analyst — adjacent entry route.
5. Real-World Evidence (RWE) Analyst — stretch target where SQL/statistics experience is explicitly required.

Do not apply to senior roles requiring independent production analytics experience unless the employer explicitly accepts freshers.

## Evidence and honesty rules

- The user's Pharm.D thesis is a prospective observational study with N=153. Use only verified thesis details and the user's actual final results; do not copy findings from the synthetic project.
- The synthetic outpatient project and synthetic antihypertensive case study are separate educational portfolio projects. Label them synthetic in every public-facing description.
- Do not claim professional analyst employment, production deployment, real-world clinical impact, remote GitHub CI success, Power BI PBIX delivery or Chromium UAT completion without evidence.
- Tool installation is not proof of proficiency. The skills matrix deliberately separates exposure, demonstrated evidence and proficiency to be assessed.
- Do not publish patient-level thesis data, institutional confidential information, or identifiable records.
