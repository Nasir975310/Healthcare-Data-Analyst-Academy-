# Client simulation: Hospital data interoperability

**Time limit:** 120 minutes. **Audience:** Hospital analytics lead and clinical informatics team.

The hospital requests a first-pass integration design for medication and laboratory analytics. Using the supplied synthetic files:
- Identify sources and define each source grain.
- Explain how HL7 v2 ADT and FHIR resources relate to the workflow.
- Create a terminology mapping review queue.
- Produce standardised curated extracts and a reconciliation report.
- Propose a warehouse fact/dimension design.
- Identify identity, security, versioning and operational risks.
- Deliver a one-page handoff with decisions, unresolved questions and acceptance evidence.

Do not claim production FHIR conformance, clinical terminology equivalence or safe patient matching from these simplified examples.
