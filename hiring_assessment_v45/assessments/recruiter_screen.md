# Stage 1 — Recruiter screen (15 minutes)

Answer aloud or in writing:
1. Give a 60-second introduction relevant to healthcare analytics.
2. Explain how your Pharm.D training informs healthcare data work without claiming clinical authority as a data analyst.
3. Describe one project you personally completed, tools used and evidence you can show.
4. Explain a gap in your experience and a concrete plan to close it.
5. What remote-work practices do you use for documentation, updates and secure data handling?

**Red flags:** generic claims, inflated experience, confusing course completion with job experience, no demonstrable project evidence.
