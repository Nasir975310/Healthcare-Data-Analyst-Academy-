# Stage 7 — Hiring-manager discussion (20 minutes)

1. Explain your strongest project in two minutes.
2. What was the data grain and how did you validate it?
3. Describe a mistake you caught (or a plausible risk you tested for).
4. What would you do if the stakeholder disputes your denominator?
5. How do you communicate an uncertain finding?
6. How do you protect PHI and confidential data while working remotely?
7. What would you learn in your first 30 days on the job?

Strong answers are specific, evidence-backed, concise and honest about limitations.
