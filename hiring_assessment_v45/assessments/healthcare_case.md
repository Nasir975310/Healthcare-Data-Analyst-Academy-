# Stage 5 — Healthcare interpretation case (25 minutes)

A synthetic observational cohort shows lower follow-up systolic BP in one treatment group, but baseline BP and comorbidity differ between groups.

Answer:
1. What is the study question and target population?
2. What is the correct patient-level grain and paired denominator?
3. Which descriptive summaries and uncertainty measures would you report?
4. What confounding by indication concerns exist?
5. What additional design/analysis might improve comparability?
6. What conclusions are not supported?

Avoid claiming efficacy or causality from an observational synthetic dataset. A medication prescription does not prove dispensing, administration or adherence.
