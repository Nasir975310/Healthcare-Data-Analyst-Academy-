# Hiring simulation scoring rubric — 100 points

| Stage | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Recruiter screen | 10 | Clear, truthful role fit and examples |
| SQL technical | 20 | Correct grain, joins, aggregations, null handling, reconciliation |
| Python/data quality | 15 | Reproducible profiling, safe cleaning, validation and logging |
| Excel/BI/dashboard | 15 | Correct KPI definitions, denominator-aware visuals and QA |
| Healthcare case interpretation | 15 | Clinical context, uncertainty, confounding and safe claims |
| Take-home assignment | 15 | Complete deliverables, tests, documentation and reproducibility |
| Hiring-manager communication | 10 | Clear explanation, ownership, limitations and prioritisation |

## Score bands
- 85–100: Strong simulated performance; still requires human review and actual work verification.
- 70–84: Competitive foundation; revise weak areas.
- 55–69: Material gaps; targeted practice required.
- Below 55: Rebuild foundations before repeating.

## Automatic critical flags
Any PHI exposure, invented results, unsafe clinical recommendation, unsupported causal inference, fabricated work experience, broken metric denominator, or unrepeatable key result triggers a fail/hold regardless of numeric score.

Self-scores are not independent assessment. Timed results are affected by familiarity, device and environment. This is practice, not hiring advice from an employer.
