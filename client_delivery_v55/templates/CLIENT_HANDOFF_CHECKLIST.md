# Client handoff checklist

- [ ] Recipient and intended use confirmed
- [ ] Data provenance and permission documented
- [ ] Minimum-necessary fields and privacy review completed
- [ ] Reporting period, grain and inclusion criteria documented
- [ ] KPI definitions and denominators approved
- [ ] Source-to-output reconciliation completed
- [ ] Missingness, duplicates and invalid values disclosed
- [ ] Limitations and non-causal interpretation stated
- [ ] Report/dashboard reviewed by a second reviewer
- [ ] Artifact inventory and SHA-256 manifest included
- [ ] Delivery channel and access expiry confirmed
- [ ] Retention/deletion responsibilities agreed
