# Source capture protocol

1. Find a vacancy through an independently chosen employer careers page or job board.
2. Record source name, exact URL, date/time found, displayed deadline, location and remote-work wording.
3. Save a short, relevant text excerpt; do not copy personal contact details or confidential messages.
4. Generate a timestamped evidence manifest and SHA-256 hash.
5. Locate the employer's official website independently and compare the vacancy details.
6. Check whether the vacancy is still open before applying; a saved snapshot can become stale.
7. Add the vacancy to the registry and record any mismatch or uncertainty.
8. Recheck deadlines and vacancy status before submitting an application.

Source evidence proves what was captured locally at a point in time, not that the content was authentic, complete or still current.
