# Three dashboard blueprints

## 1. Executive overview
Cards: Encounter Count, Unique Patients, IPD Share, Total Charges (INR).
Visuals: monthly encounters line chart; department encounters bar chart; IPD/OPD split; charges by department.
Slicers: date, department, encounter type. Display refresh date and metric definitions.

## 2. Clinical operations
Cards: Encounter Count, IPD Encounters, Average IPD Length of Stay.
Visuals: monthly department workload matrix; LOS by department; discharge status distribution.
Do not label LOS as a quality outcome without risk adjustment and case-mix context.

## 3. Pharmacy services
Cards: Medication Review Encounters, Unique Patients.
Visuals: monthly medication review workload and department referral source.
The sample contains encounter-level medication review activity only. It does not support drug utilisation, adherence, medication safety, intervention acceptance or clinical outcome claims.
