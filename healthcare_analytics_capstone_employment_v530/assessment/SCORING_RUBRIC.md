# Employer-style assessment rubric (100 points)

| Area | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Data profiling and quality | 20 | Counts, types, missingness, duplicate keys, orphan detection and severity |
| Data model and SQL | 20 | Correct grain, PK/FK design, no fact-to-fact fanout, reproducible queries |
| KPI correctness | 15 | Explicit definitions, correct denominators, reconciliation |
| Dashboard design | 15 | Clear hierarchy, appropriate chart choice, filters and accessibility |
| Clinical/data governance | 10 | Synthetic-data statement, cautious interpretation, privacy and security |
| Communication | 10 | Concise executive narrative, caveats, recommendations |
| Reproducibility and handover | 10 | Run instructions, dependencies, versioning, QA and known limitations |

Suggested pass threshold: 70/100. Strong portfolio submission: 85/100 or above. A high score does not establish professional competency or clinical authority.
