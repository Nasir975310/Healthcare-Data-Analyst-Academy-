# Volume 5 integrated clinical and pharmacy analytics capstone

## Objective
Build a validated, reproducible descriptive analytics package using four linked synthetic tables: patients, encounters, medication orders and laboratory results.

## Required work
1. Profile all four files and validate keys, date logic and joins.
2. Describe encounters by department and distinct patient counts.
3. Summarise length of stay and discharge status, with distribution-aware reporting.
4. Describe medication orders by class, route and generic-name documentation flag.
5. Profile laboratory results by analyte, unit and status. Do not pool incompatible analytes or units.
6. Create a data model diagram or written grain specification.
7. Produce a short report and at least three charts/tables.
8. State what the data cannot answer.

## Required caution
The files are synthetic and intentionally contain one-to-many relationships. They do not represent actual clinical practice. No clinical thresholds, treatment effectiveness or hospital quality rankings should be inferred. There is no expected-test table, so laboratory completion rates cannot be calculated.
