# Volume 4 capstone — Synthetic cohort and biostatistics

## Purpose
Practise research-question specification, descriptive analysis, group comparisons, regression concepts, missing-data review and evidence communication.

## Data
`data/synthetic_biostatistics_cohort.csv` contains 240 entirely synthetic patient-level records. No real patient data are included. Variables: patient_id, age_years, diabetes (0/1), regimen_group, discharge_sbp, followup_days, readmission_30d (0/1), and site.

## Questions
1. Describe the cohort and missingness in discharge SBP.
2. Estimate mean discharge SBP by regimen group with an appropriate uncertainty interval.
3. Compare the 30-day readmission proportions by regimen group; report absolute and relative measures with uncertainty.
4. Fit an exploratory adjusted model using age and diabetes, then explain why the observational association is not a causal treatment effect.
5. Discuss whether a time-to-event analysis is justified. The supplied followup_days field is not an event-time variable and must not be misused as one.

## Required deliverables
- One-page protocol/analysis specification.
- Data dictionary and validation log.
- Reproducible script or notebook.
- Table 1, missingness table and one suitable figure.
- Short report with estimates, confidence intervals, limitations and reproducibility instructions.

## Critical cautions
This is a teaching dataset generated by code. Its associations are artificial. It is not suitable for clinical recommendations, treatment comparisons or publication. Do not infer drug effectiveness. Do not treat the dataset as a survival dataset.
