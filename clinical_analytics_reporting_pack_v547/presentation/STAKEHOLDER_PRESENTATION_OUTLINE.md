# Stakeholder presentation outline (8 slides)

1. **Question & scope** — what the fictional quality team asked; descriptive review only.
2. **Data and grain** — one synthetic record per unique record ID; 301 raw rows, 300 unique IDs.
3. **Data quality** — duplicate, missing follow-up BP, missing class and implausible value handling.
4. **Utilization profile** — primary drug class and regimen complexity; display denominator and missingness.
5. **Follow-up BP** — paired SBP changes with sample sizes and 95% CIs.
6. **Statistical interpretation** — paired-change tests and exploratory ANOVA; assumptions and no causal inference.
7. **Limitations** — synthetic data, complete-pair selection, unadjusted comparisons, no dose/duration/titration detail.
8. **Recommendations** — improve follow-up capture, standardize definitions and design a properly governed real-world study.

Speaker rule: Never say one class is more effective based on this analysis. Clearly label every result as synthetic.
