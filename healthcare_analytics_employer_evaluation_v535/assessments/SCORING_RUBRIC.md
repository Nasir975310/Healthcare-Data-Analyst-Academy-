# Employer-style assessment rubric — 100 points

| Competency | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| SQL and relational reasoning | 20 | Correct grain, joins, aggregation, nulls and duplicate handling |
| Python and reproducibility | 15 | Rerunnable code, explicit schema, deterministic outputs |
| Data quality and reconciliation | 15 | Raw/accepted/rejected counts, exceptions and provenance |
| Healthcare interpretation | 10 | Correctly distinguishes orders, encounters and outcomes |
| BI and KPI design | 15 | Star schema, validated measures, clear denominators |
| Privacy, security and governance | 10 | Synthetic/licensed data, least privilege, secret hygiene |
| Communication | 10 | Concise findings, caveats and audience-appropriate explanation |
| Professional delivery | 5 | README, tests, versioning and clean handover |

## Readiness interpretation
- 90–100: strong evidence for junior analyst interviews; employer onboarding still required.
- 80–89: interview-ready for selected junior roles; remediate weaker domains.
- 70–79: developing; targeted practice recommended.
- 60–69: foundational gaps remain.
- Below 60: repeat core modules and practical challenge.

## Hard gates
Fabricated experience, exposed credentials/PHI, materially false clinical claims, or inability to explain submitted work means **not ready**, regardless of numeric score. This is a practice indicator, not certification or a hiring guarantee.
