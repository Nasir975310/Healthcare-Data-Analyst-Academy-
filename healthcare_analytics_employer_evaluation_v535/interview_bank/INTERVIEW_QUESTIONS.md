# Junior Healthcare Data Analyst interview bank

## SQL
1. Difference between WHERE and HAVING?
2. How do NULL values affect COUNT, joins and filters?
3. Explain primary key, foreign key and referential integrity.
4. What is a window function? Give a healthcare operations example.
5. Why can joining two one-to-many facts inflate totals?
6. How would you find duplicates and orphan records?

## Python and data quality
7. How do you validate a CSV before loading it?
8. When would you use a set, dictionary or DataFrame?
9. How do you make an ETL pipeline idempotent?
10. How do you log and quarantine invalid rows?
11. How would you compare two large extracts efficiently?

## Statistics and healthcare
12. Difference between prevalence and incidence?
13. Why does association not imply causation?
14. Explain confidence interval versus p-value.
15. What is the denominator for a readmission rate?
16. Why are medication orders not equivalent to adherence?
17. How would missing-not-at-random data affect interpretation?

## BI and delivery
18. Explain a star schema and fact-table grain.
19. How would you validate a DAX measure?
20. What is row-level security and what can it not replace?
21. How do you investigate a slow report?
22. What belongs in a data dictionary?
23. How do you communicate a data-quality issue to a nontechnical manager?

## Integrity and ownership
24. Which parts of your portfolio did you personally execute?
25. Which tools did you use, and which integrations remain untested?
26. How do you ensure no PHI or secrets enter Git?
27. What would you do if asked to remove inconvenient records without documenting why?
28. What did you get wrong initially, and how did you correct it?

Use STAR for behavioral questions. Never invent employment, client outcomes or platform experience.
