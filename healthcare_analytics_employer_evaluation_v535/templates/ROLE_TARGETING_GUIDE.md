# Entry-level role targeting guide

Prioritize roles aligned with demonstrated skills:
- Junior Healthcare Data Analyst
- Clinical Data Analyst / Clinical Research Data Assistant
- Hospital Operations / Quality Data Analyst
- Pharmacy Data Analyst / Drug Utilization Analyst
- Junior BI Analyst in healthcare
- Pharmacovigilance data operations roles when domain requirements match

For each posting, extract required tools, domain knowledge, experience threshold, location/timezone, employment type and portfolio expectations. Score skill match honestly. Do not apply to roles requiring clinical licensure or senior production ownership unless the posting explicitly permits junior applicants.

Avoid paying for job placement, sharing OTPs, or sending identity/bank documents before verifying the employer and process.
