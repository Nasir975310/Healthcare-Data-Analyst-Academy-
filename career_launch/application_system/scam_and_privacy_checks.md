# Recruitment, scam and privacy checks

- Never pay a recruiter, employer or intermediary for a job offer, interview slot or mandatory equipment deposit without independently verifying a legitimate arrangement.
- Verify the domain, legal employer identity, recruiter identity and requisition on the employer's official careers site.
- Treat requests for OTPs, banking passwords, remote access to personal devices, cryptocurrency or urgent payment as red flags.
- Do not share Aadhaar/PAN/bank details before verifying the employer and the legitimate onboarding stage. Share only what is legally required through secure official channels.
- Do not upload patient data, clinical source documents, confidential trial data or employer datasets into public portfolios or personal AI tools.
- Do not place passwords, API keys or tokens in GitHub repositories.
- Read internship duration, stipend, conversion terms, training fees, working hours, bond and contract clauses carefully.
- Job-board copies can be stale or inaccurate; confirm live status and eligibility with the employer.
