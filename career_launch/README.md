# Career Launch System — v3.0.0

## Included
- Role maps for healthcare/hospital analytics, clinical data management, pharmacovigilance, RWE/HEOR and healthcare vendor analytics.
- Dated India job-market snapshot with public posting examples and caveats.
- CV, LinkedIn, cover letter, job-description scorecard and application tracker templates.
- Five timed hiring simulations (SQL, Excel/reporting, Python/data quality, healthcare interpretation, remote client delivery) with 100-point rubrics.
- Twelve career launch challenges.
- Application workflow, 30-day earn-while-learn execution plan and recruitment scam/privacy checks.
- Local challenge completion tracking, included in full Academy progress backup export/import.

## Career strategy
Apply to entry-level and adjacent roles while building portfolio evidence. Do not wait until every curriculum topic is complete. Prioritise roles where degree eligibility and experience requirements fit; target remote, hybrid and on-site roles depending on constraints.

## Market snapshot
The market snapshot is dated 2026-11-02 and based on a small set of public postings and one third-party aggregate. It is illustrative, not a comprehensive labour-market census or a verified live vacancy feed. Confirm every role on the employer's official careers page.

## Honesty and privacy
Do not describe Academy projects as employment. Clearly label synthetic datasets. Never claim production EDC, MedDRA, CDISC conformance, Power BI, claims/EHR or other system experience unless actually obtained. Never publish PHI, confidential trial data, employer data, secrets or personal identifiers.
