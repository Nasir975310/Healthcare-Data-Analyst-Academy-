# Analyst assignment — 90 minutes

## Scenario
A fictional hospital quality team wants a descriptive review of antihypertensive prescribing patterns and follow-up BP documentation. You are asked to produce an auditable analysis, not a treatment recommendation.

## Deliverables
1. Data quality report with counts and issue categories.
2. SQL output for all four supplied queries.
3. One table comparing paired SBP summaries by primary drug class.
4. A 1-page report with 3 findings, 3 limitations and 2 data-improvement recommendations.
5. A short verbal explanation of why the results cannot establish comparative drug efficacy.

## Suggested workflow
- 10 min: inspect schema, grain, missingness and duplicates.
- 15 min: validate and reconcile counts.
- 20 min: execute SQL and inspect outputs.
- 25 min: interpret descriptive results and limitations.
- 20 min: prepare stakeholder summary.

## Assessment rubric (100 points)
- Data understanding and grain: 15
- Data quality and reconciliation: 20
- SQL correctness: 20
- Descriptive analysis: 15
- Clinical interpretation and restraint: 20
- Communication and reproducibility: 10

Automatic review required if the submission claims causality, ranks drug efficacy, treats the illustrative threshold as a universal target, or presents synthetic findings as real hospital evidence.
