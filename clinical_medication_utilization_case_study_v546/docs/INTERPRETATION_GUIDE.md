# Interpretation guide

A sound analyst distinguishes observed data from inference. Mean changes among complete pairs describe only records with usable baseline and follow-up SBP. They do not measure an individual treatment response or prove a drug class is more effective. Differences may reflect confounding by indication, age, comorbidity, baseline severity, dose, combination therapy, adherence, follow-up timing and missingness.

The synthetic data-generating process is intentionally simplified. Do not use it to teach clinical prescribing. The <140/90 flag is only a demonstration of a configurable binary metric; actual BP targets depend on patient context and current clinical guidance.
