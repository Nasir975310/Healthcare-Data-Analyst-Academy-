# Target role profiles — India and remote markets

These are archetypes for practice, not claims about currently open vacancies or guaranteed hiring requirements. Verify each employer's actual requirements before applying.

## 1. Junior Healthcare Data Analyst
**Typical work:** extract, clean, validate and summarize operational or clinical datasets; maintain recurring reports; investigate discrepancies; document metric definitions.

**Core skills:** Excel, SQL, Power BI, data quality, descriptive statistics, communication.

**Evidence to show:** one end-to-end synthetic hospital project, reproducible SQL, a dashboard, QA evidence and a concise business summary.

## 2. Clinical Data Analyst / Research Data Assistant
**Typical work:** study data cleaning, visit and query tracking, descriptive tables, protocol-aligned reporting and documentation.

**Core skills:** Excel, SQL, research methods, data dictionaries, GCP awareness, R or Python as required.

**Evidence to show:** synthetic study dataset, data validation log, Table 1, analysis plan and limitations.

## 3. Pharmacy / Medication Utilization Analyst
**Typical work:** medication-use summaries, formulary reporting, prescription data quality and utilization indicators under defined governance.

**Core skills:** pharmacy domain knowledge, SQL, Excel, statistics, terminology awareness and careful interpretation.

**Evidence to show:** synthetic medication utilization analysis with clear denominators and no unsupported clinical conclusions.

## 4. Pharmacovigilance Data Associate / Safety Data Analyst
**Typical work:** case intake quality, duplicate screening support, coding and aggregate safety reporting under validated SOPs.

**Core skills:** pharmacovigilance fundamentals, structured data, MedDRA awareness, Excel, quality systems and documentation.

**Evidence to show:** synthetic case-processing workflow and quality checks. Do not imply independent signal detection or regulated-system qualification without experience.

## 5. Healthcare BI Analyst
**Typical work:** semantic models, dashboard development, KPI governance, refresh monitoring and stakeholder reporting.

**Core skills:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query, SQL, dimensional modelling, RLS and performance tuning.

**Evidence to show:** documented star schema, tested measures, role test plan and dashboard acceptance criteria.

## Role-selection guidance
Prioritize roles where the job description matches skills you can demonstrate now. A Pharm.D degree is relevant domain context, but it does not replace analytics evidence. Avoid claiming production EHR, claims, FHIR, regulatory or cloud experience unless actually performed.
