# Structured interview preparation

## 60-second introduction framework
1. Pharm.D and healthcare-domain foundation.
2. Transition into analytics and the specific tools learned.
3. One project: problem, data, method, QA and outcome.
4. Role fit and what you can contribute.

Do not claim professional experience for coursework or synthetic projects. Label academic and portfolio work accurately.

## Behavioural questions
- Describe a data error you found and how you traced it.
- Tell me about a time you had to explain a technical issue to a non-technical stakeholder.
- How do you handle unclear requirements?
- What do you do when a deadline is at risk?
- How do you protect confidential data while working remotely?

Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Include measurable outcomes only when actually measured.

## Technical discussion prompts
- Explain the grain of your project tables.
- Demonstrate a SQL join and explain its cardinality.
- Explain one DAX measure and its filter context.
- Show how you reconciled source and output row counts.
- Explain one limitation of your clinical analysis.
- Explain how another analyst can reproduce your work.

## Questions to ask the employer
- Which source systems and data domains does the team use?
- How are metric definitions governed?
- What does a successful first 90 days look like?
- What training and review processes are available?
- What are the access-control and data-handling requirements?
