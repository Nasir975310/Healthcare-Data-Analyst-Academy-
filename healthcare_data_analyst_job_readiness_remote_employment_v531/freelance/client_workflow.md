# Freelance healthcare analytics: safe delivery workflow

## Before accepting
- Clarify business question, source, ownership, permitted purpose and authorized access.
- Confirm whether data contain personal or health information.
- Establish lawful basis, contractual terms, confidentiality, retention and deletion requirements with the client.
- Do not download or transfer identifiable patient data to personal devices or unapproved services.
- Decline work if access, authorization, scope or safeguards are unclear.

## Scope and estimate
Write down deliverables, exclusions, source assumptions, refresh frequency, review rounds, acceptance criteria, timeline and dependencies. Estimate discovery, profiling, build, validation, documentation and handover separately.

## Delivery stages
1. Discovery and data access approval.
2. Source inventory and data profiling.
3. Agreed metric definitions.
4. Build in an approved environment.
5. Independent reconciliation and QA.
6. Client review and correction.
7. Final handover, access revocation and retention/deletion confirmation.

## Pricing discipline
Do not use a universal hourly rate. Estimate hours and risk, research local market rates, account for platform fees/tax, and quote a bounded scope. Avoid unpaid large test projects. Never promise clinical or regulatory outcomes from a dashboard.
