# Job-description human review checklist

- [ ] Employer legal name and official website independently confirmed
- [ ] Vacancy exists on the employer's official careers page or verified recruiter channel
- [ ] Job title, employment type, location and remote eligibility are explicit
- [ ] Salary range and payment arrangement are plausible and documented
- [ ] Mandatory qualifications separated from preferred qualifications
- [ ] Experience requirement is realistic for entry-level candidacy
- [ ] Tools listed are mapped to demonstrable evidence
- [ ] No recruitment, registration, deposit or training fee requested
- [ ] No OTP, password, card CVV, banking credential or unnecessary identity document requested
- [ ] CV claims can be supported by thesis, portfolio, coursework or employment evidence
- [ ] Application decision and reason entered in application tracker
