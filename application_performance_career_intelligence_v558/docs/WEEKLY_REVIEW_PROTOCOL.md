# Weekly application review protocol

Review a rolling 4-week period, but retain the underlying row-level tracker.

1. Check duplicate IDs, invalid dates and missing stages before interpreting rates.
2. Compare the number of saved, submitted, screening, assessment, interview, offer, rejected and withdrawn applications.
3. Inspect rejection reasons. Separate eligibility barriers (location, shifts, work authorisation, required experience) from skill gaps and unknown reasons.
4. Compare CV versions only when role types and application periods are reasonably comparable. Small counts are descriptive, not evidence of causal improvement.
5. Choose one improvement for the next week: target role selection, truthful evidence ordering, portfolio proof, interview practice or vacancy sourcing.
6. Record the change and review after enough additional applications; do not overreact to one or two outcomes.

## Practical guardrails

- Do not share OTPs, passwords, bank credentials or unnecessary identity documents.
- Verify the employer through an independently located official site and contact channel.
- Never pay an application, interview, equipment-release or guaranteed-placement fee.
- Do not put patient-level data or confidential employer information into this tracker.
