# Five-minute portfolio walkthrough

**0:00–0:40 — Business question**  
State the fictional stakeholder, decision question, population, time window and intended output.

**0:40–1:20 — Data and grain**  
Explain each table's grain, source provenance, synthetic status, key relationships and important exclusions.

**1:20–2:20 — Method**  
Show SQL/Python workflow, transformations, metric definitions and why those choices fit the question.

**2:20–3:20 — QA and exceptions**  
Show input/output counts, duplicate handling, missingness, rejected records, reconciliation and tests. Explain one defect and its resolution.

**3:20–4:20 — Findings and limitations**  
Present only results that can be reproduced. State uncertainty, missing context, non-causal interpretation and what the data cannot answer.

**4:20–5:00 — Handover**  
Show how to reproduce, where outputs live, what remains unverified and the next question you would ask the stakeholder.

## Reviewer scorecard (20 points)
- Business question and audience: 0–4
- Data grain and metric clarity: 0–4
- Reproducibility and QA: 0–4
- Interpretation and limitations: 0–4
- Communication and handover: 0–4

Target: 15/20 with no privacy/integrity failure. Rehearse twice and improve the lowest-scoring section.
