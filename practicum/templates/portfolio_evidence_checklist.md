# Practicum portfolio evidence checklist

- [ ] Sanitised client brief / synthetic scenario
- [ ] Clarification questions and approved assumptions
- [ ] Metric dictionary and grain/denominator contract
- [ ] Data inventory, source hashes and data dictionary
- [ ] Reproducible SQL/Python/Excel/BI artefacts
- [ ] Data-quality issue log and reconciliation
- [ ] Independent calculation or peer review
- [ ] Executive summary with evidence and limitations
- [ ] Client handoff and reproduction instructions
- [ ] README, environment and version history
- [ ] Reflection: what changed after review

Do not publish identifiable patient information, confidential client extracts, screenshots of EHRs or credentials.
