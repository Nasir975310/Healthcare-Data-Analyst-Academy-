# PRAC-02 — Antihypertensive DUE: starter pack

**Client:** Clinical pharmacy and therapeutics committee  
**Timebox:** 5 working days

Define the DUE cohort before calculating. State whether the analytical unit is encounter, patient or order line. Filter `drug_class = Antihypertensive` for the supplied training extract. Summarise generic-name order patterns and missingness. For BP change, use records with both baseline and follow-up SBP and report the paired n.

**Do not** claim order records demonstrate administration, dispensing or adherence. Do not call an observed BP difference a treatment effect. Do not claim guideline concordance unless you identify the dated guideline, inclusion criteria, contraindications and source variables required.
