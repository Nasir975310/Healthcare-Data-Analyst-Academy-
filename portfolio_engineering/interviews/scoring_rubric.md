# Interview scoring rubric (100 points)

| Dimension | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical correctness | 25 | Correct SQL/Python/statistical reasoning |
| Data reasoning | 20 | Grain, keys, denominators, missingness, validation |
| Healthcare interpretation | 15 | Clinical semantics and no unsupported inference |
| Communication | 15 | Clear, concise explanation and clarification |
| Engineering practice | 15 | Reproducibility, tests, version control |
| Privacy and governance | 10 | Safe handling, least privilege, approved systems |

Suggested practice bands: 85–100 strong; 70–84 developing; 55–69 targeted remediation; below 55 revisit fundamentals. These bands are training guidance only, not hiring cut-offs.
