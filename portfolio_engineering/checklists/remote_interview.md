# Remote interview and client delivery rehearsal

- [ ] Can I introduce my role and strongest relevant project in 60–90 seconds?
- [ ] Can I explain the grain and key relationships without opening a notebook?
- [ ] Can I explain why a join can inflate counts?
- [ ] Can I define a KPI numerator, denominator, period and exclusions?
- [ ] Can I describe one defect I found and how I validated the fix?
- [ ] Can I explain one limitation that changes interpretation?
- [ ] Can I clarify an ambiguous request professionally?
- [ ] Can I explain how I manage deadlines, blockers and async updates?
- [ ] Can I show a clean reproducible run and version history?
- [ ] Can I describe privacy controls and what I would do after accidental receipt of PHI?
