# Privacy and disclosure-risk review

- [ ] Purpose and lawful/approved access confirmed
- [ ] Minimum necessary fields selected
- [ ] Direct identifiers removed where not required
- [ ] Quasi-identifiers and linkage risk assessed
- [ ] Small-cell suppression/aggregation considered
- [ ] Free-text fields reviewed for identifiers
- [ ] Recipient, channel and retention confirmed
- [ ] Re-identification and onward-sharing risks assessed
- [ ] Institutional policy and applicable law reviewed by responsible owner

This checklist is not a legal determination or de-identification certification.
