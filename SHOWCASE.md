# Healthcare Data Analyst Academy — showcase

**Packaged baseline: v5.60.3.** This is an offline-first educational repository and portfolio toolkit, not a production SaaS platform.

## Included
- Learning material for Excel, SQL, Python, statistics, research methods, data quality, ETL, databases, healthcare data engineering, BI and reporting.
- Practice modules, exercises, capstones, scripts, datasets, dictionaries, metric contracts and tests.
- Synthetic outpatient and medication-utilisation analytics projects with non-clinical-use safeguards.
- Portfolio reproducibility, QA, publication-readiness and evidence-capture tools.
- Career materials: CV/LinkedIn templates, role-fit evaluation, interview preparation, application tracking, vacancy verification and evidence capture.

## Suggested route
1. Excel/SQL foundations.
2. Python and pandas analysis.
3. Healthcare data quality and statistics.
4. Synthetic outpatient analytics capstone.
5. Reproducible report and portfolio case study.
6. Career launch and evidence-based applications.

## Evidence boundaries
The maintained career test suites have a release-time record of passing. The historical audit discovered 69 test directories and remains incomplete: 33 passed, 16 timed out, 2 collected no tests and 18 were not run. Browser UAT is pending. No live vacancies, employer verification, applications, external review, clinical deployment or recruiter contact is claimed.

The real Pharm.D thesis is separate from synthetic projects. Never merge them or present fictional results as clinical findings.
