## Important interpretation caveats
- Dataset and event indicator are simulated; no clinical treatment effect or risk estimate is supported.
- Holdout logistic model is a teaching demonstration, not a validated prediction model. At a 0.50 threshold the rare-event sample may yield no positive predictions; inspect calibration and threshold trade-offs.
- The synthetic dataset is not intended for sample-size justification or real protocol design.
- Statistical outputs are not clinical advice or decision support.
