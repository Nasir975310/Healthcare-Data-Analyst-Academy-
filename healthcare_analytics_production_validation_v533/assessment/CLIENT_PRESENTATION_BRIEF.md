# Client-style presentation (8–10 minutes)

Present to a fictional hospital operations manager:
1. Business question and scope (1 min).
2. Data sources, grain and pipeline (2 min).
3. Three descriptive findings with denominators (2 min).
4. Dashboard walkthrough and KPI definitions (2 min).
5. Quality exceptions, risks and mitigations (1 min).
6. Limitations, next steps and acceptance request (1–2 min).

Do not claim causal effects, clinical outcomes, adherence or operational savings unless measured by a valid design. Explain the deliberately rejected orphan encounter.
